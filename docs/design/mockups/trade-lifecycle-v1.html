2 · Stop loss and target, run by HapieCoin

A new step in the trade flow, after Preview and before the order is sent. Off by default. The same panel opens later from the card (“Protect”), from Details and from the workbench alert line. Nothing is sent to the exchange for this: HapieCoin watches the strategy's P&L and sends this strategy's exit orders when a level is crossed.

ModePreviewProtectPlace

Protect BTC-SSTG-11SEP26-1432

Exit every leg of this strategy when its P&L crosses a level. Only this strategy's lots are sent; other strategies on the same strikes are untouched.

Stop loss · exit when P&L ≤
−₹5,000 · 62% of max loss · about 1.4× the credit
Target · exit when P&L ≥
+₹4,200 · 50% of max profit (the usual credit-strategy exit)
Checkedby the HapieCoin server, and by this browser while a tab is open
What this is and is not. It is a rule on HapieCoin's server, not an order at the exchange. If HapieCoin is down at that moment nothing fires. Exits go as market orders at the mark of that second; in a fast market the fill can be worse than the level. You get a Telegram and in-app message when it fires. An exchange-level stop is the only hard guarantee, but it acts on the whole position at the exchange, across every strategy that shares the contract.

3 · Overlap warning before an order

Shown in the paper and live Preview, and in the workbench Review, whenever a leg's contract is already held in another open strategy. It never blocks: the trader may intend it.
