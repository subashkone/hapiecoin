4 · Who builds these screens

  • Coinglass — the template for the hub, overview, liquidations and ETF pages; 30+ exchanges, a derived liquidation heatmap, ETF flows.
  • Hyblock Capital — trader-facing liquidation heatmaps and derivatives indicators; free tier limited to majors.
  • Velo Data — free liquidation and open-interest dashboards aimed at institutions.
  • Laevitas — options-first analytics (term structure, skew, max pain); API-first, enterprise pricing.
  • CryptoQuant — exchange flows, whale ratio, Coinbase premium.
  • Glassnode — on-chain metrics such as reserves and the Puell multiple.
  • Farside Investors — the canonical daily ETF flow table.
  • alternative.me — the Fear & Greed index and its free API.
  • Deribit Insights — the max-pain method with code.
  • Hyperliquid leaderboard and copy-trading tools — all built on the public info API.

5 · How the data would flow

Adapters
One per source in a new apps/ingest service: schedule, rate budget, normaliser to packages/schema
Store
Redis snapshot per dataset with a TTL; Postgres for history beyond the provider window (Binance stats: 30 days)
Serve
GET /v1/analytics/{dataset} on the API with cache headers and an asOf stamp on every payload
Show
Every chart prints its source and "as of"; a stale snapshot shows a badge, never an empty chart
Cadence
Liquidations live · OI / funding / L/S 60 s · spot 5 min · F&G hourly · cycle indicators daily
  • Provider keys only in the environment; adapters never run in the browser. Every browser request stays on our domain.
  • Aggregation is stated in the UI as "Aggregated across Binance · OKX · Bybit", never "all exchanges".
  • Swapping a source later touches one adapter, not the pages.

6 · Terms of use to respect

  • Exchange public market data is free to consume; check each venue's redistribution and attribution wording before launch.
  • CoinGecko Demo requires attribution and is meant for low-volume or non-commercial use; Basic ($35/mo) is the safe commercial floor.
  • Coinglass commercial use starts at the Standard tier.
  • Farside has no official API; do not scrape it for a paid product without permission.

7 · Open points for ADR-038

  • Confirm Deribit and Hyperliquid unauthenticated rate limits from their docs before sizing refresh intervals.
  • CoinGecko: start on Demo with 10-minute refreshes, or buy Basic at launch to lift the 10k/month cap.
  • "Coming soon" panels for ETF flows, exchange balances and unlocks in v1 — agreed.
  • Budget ceiling zero for now — agreed; revisit with usage data.
