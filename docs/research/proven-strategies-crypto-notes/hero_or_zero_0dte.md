## Key question 1: What is "hero or zero" in Indian index options, what do the SEBI studies say, and what regulatory changes followed?

### Takeaway
"Hero or zero" (also "zero to hero") is a retail practice of buying very cheap, far out-of-the-money Nifty / Bank Nifty options on the weekly expiry day hoping for a many-fold intraday payoff; every SEBI study from FY22 through FY26 finds roughly 88-93% of individual F&O traders lose money net of costs, with options (overwhelmingly index options traded on or within a day of expiry) accounting for about 92% of individual losses, and SEBI's October 2024 package cut weekly expiries to one index per exchange, tripled minimum contract size and added an expiry-day extreme-loss margin on short options.

### Cited Findings

**What the practice is (all [blog/forum] or broker education pages)**
- A Fyers community thread (Nov 2022) defines it as buying cheap options close to expiry hoping they finish in the money; one poster's rules: trade only on expiry day, buy not sell, do not pay more than roughly Rs 500-600 per contract, 2-3 lots at most, target at least 1:4 of contract value. The worked example: a Nifty 18450 CE bought at Rs 2.65 at the open reached Rs 34 after a late-session rally; the poster said a loss "would have been only about Rs 300" and that it works "but not every time". - Broker education pages describe the same bet: if the market moves sharply the option becomes a "hero", if range-bound deep OTM options go to "zero"; premiums are cheap so participation needs small capital. — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/learning-center/futures-and-options/expiry-trading-strategies-risk-and-opportunities-in-the-stock-market/article-1536/), [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/futures-and-options/expiry-day-option-buying-strategy)

**Regulatory response: SEBI circular of 1 October 2024 (six measures) [regulator, via broker summary]**
- Index-derivative contract value raised to Rs 15-20 lakh (from Rs 5-10 lakh) for new contracts from 21 November 2024; weekly expiries limited to one benchmark index per exchange from 20 November 2024 (NSE kept Nifty, BSE kept Sensex; Bank Nifty, Finnifty, Midcap Nifty, Bankex weeklies discontinued); full option premium collected upfront; no calendar-spread margin benefit on expiry day from 10 February 2025; intraday position-limit monitoring from 1 April 2025; an additional 2% Extreme Loss Margin on short option positions on expiry day from 20 November 2024. — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/sebis-new-rules-for-index-derivatives-heres-whats-changing), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-announces-six-key-changes-to-curb-speculation-in-derivatives-trading-124100101316_1.html)
- After the measures, options participation fell 25.8% between Q2 and Q4 FY25 (index options 26.8%) while turnover per remaining index-options trader rose 12%. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/rs91685-cr-lost-88-of-individual-traders-lost-money-in-fy26-options-drove-92-of-losses-550474-2026-08-21)

**Expiry-day manipulation case (context for who is on the other side of hero-or-zero) [regulator, via legal blogs and press]**
- SEBI's 105-page interim order of 3 July 2025 alleged that Jane Street entities, on 18 Bank Nifty expiry days between January 2023 and March 2025 (plus three Nifty days), bought Bank Nifty constituents and stock futures in the morning (sometimes over 20% of market-wide volume in Kotak Bank, SBI, Axis Bank) while building large short-call / long-put index option positions, then sold the stock and futures aggressively from about 11:49 AM into the close; SEBI impounded Rs 4,843.57 crore of alleged unlawful gains. - A second pattern (May 2025 expiries) reversed the direction: Jane Street carried a Rs 44,154 crore short Bank Nifty options position into the close and booked Rs 225 crore in a day from the soft close. — [Moneylife explainer](https://moneylife.in/article/explainer-what-are-sebis-findings-on-jane-street-groups-market-manipulation/77594.html)

### Inferences
- The SEBI series shows no evidence that the typical retail expiry-day option buyer has positive expectancy: the loss share has stayed between 88% and 93% across five fiscal years despite a change of brokers sampled and despite the 2024 curbs, and about 60-70% of individual index-option turnover is on expiry day itself.
- The 2026 SEBI numbers imply that even before costs 82% lose, so the hero-or-zero loss is not purely a cost artefact; costs then take roughly a third of gross losses.
- The Jane Street order documents that on Bank Nifty expiry days a large algorithmic participant was positioned short options while moving the index intraday; whether or not manipulation is proven, it illustrates that the retail buyer's counterparty is an algorithmic seller (96-97% of prop/FPI profits were algorithmic per SEBI 2024).

### Gaps
- No SEBI study isolates the P&L of far-OTM option buyers on expiry day specifically; the "expiry-day share of turnover" is the closest regulator statistic.
- No peer-reviewed or regulator backtest of a hero-or-zero rule set (e.g. buy Rs 1-5 strikes at 1 PM on expiry) was found; forum analyses report index-move statistics but no option P&L.
- The SEBI September 2024 press-release body (per-year shares, cost breakdown) could not be fetched from sebi.gov.in; figures come from press reproductions.

## Key question 2: Academic evidence on deep-OTM / lottery option returns and 0DTE-specific studies; what did retail 0DTE buyers earn?

### Takeaway
Every major peer-reviewed study finds that far out-of-the-money, positively skewed options earn strongly negative average returns (OTM single-stock calls down to roughly -37% per month at the highest moneyness in Ni's sample; skewness-sorted option-portfolio spreads of 10-50% per week in Boyer-Vorkink; ATM straddles -3% per week in Coval-Shumway), and the only study of retail 0DTE P&L (Beckmeyer-Branger-Gayda, Cboe SPX data, Jan 2021-Feb 2023) finds aggregate retail losses of roughly $70-125 million with the daily loss roughly doubling once daily expirations began in May 2022; 0DTE options carry the largest volatility risk premium of any tenor in Dim-Eraker-Vilkov.

### Cited Findings

**Classic option-return literature [peer-reviewed]**
- Coval and Shumway, "Expected Option Returns", Journal of Finance 2001: zero-beta at-the-money S&P 500 straddles lose on average about 3% per week, robust to nonsynchronous trading and sample changes; a later replication reproduces -3.15% per week. — [EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jfinan:v:56:y:2001:i:3:p:983-1009), [Cambridge supplement](https://static.cambridge.org/content/id/urn%3Acambridge.org%3Aid%3Aarticle%3AS0022109024000309/resource/name/S0022109024000309sup001.pdf)
- Bondarenko, "Why Are Put Options So Expensive?", Quarterly Journal of Finance 2014: the "overpriced puts puzzle" (historical S&P 500 put prices too high for canonical models); a model-free test shows no model in the class studied (allowing for Peso problems and biased beliefs) can explain the anomaly. Only the abstract was readable; sample period and returns by moneyness not visible. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wsi/qjfxxx/v04y2014i03ns2010139214500153.html)
- Boyer and Vorkink, "Stock Options as Lotteries", Journal of Finance 2014: total skewness has a strong negative relation with average option returns; differences in average returns across ex-ante-skewness-sorted option portfolios "range from 10% to 50% per week, even after controlling for risk"; the 2011 draft covered roughly 1996-2009 and its summary states returns ignore transaction fees, so net returns would be lower. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v69y2014i4p1485-1527.html), [CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/?p=13232)
- Ni, "Stock Option Returns: A Puzzle" (1996-2005, US single-stock calls): OTM calls have negative average returns and high-strike calls earn lower returns than low-strike calls; a secondary reproduction of the tables shows one-month midpoint returns of about +2.3% for the lowest-moneyness bucket falling to about -36.9% for moneyness above 1.15, with only ITM calls positive; using ask prices instead of midpoints may eliminate the positive ATM/ITM returns. — [HKUST research portal](https://researchportal.hkust.edu.hk/en/publications/stock-option-returns-a-puzzle/), [Tomasik summary](https://reducing-suffering.org/do-call-options-have-high-expected-returns)
- Bali and Murray, "Does Risk-Neutral Skewness Predict the Cross-Section of Equity Option Portfolio Returns?", JFQA 2013 (monthly options 1996-2010): a strong negative relation between risk-neutral skewness and returns of delta- and vega-neutral "skewness assets", not explained by market, size, value, momentum, reversal, volatility or option-market factors, "consistent with a preference for positively skewed assets". — [Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/does-riskneutral-skewness-predict-the-crosssection-of-equity-option-portfolio-returns/EEA6370E45B2B3C10139A60A5AEC1DAD), [OptionMetrics summary](https://optionmetrics.com/research/t-g-bali-and-s-murrary-does-risk-neutral-skewness-predict-the-cross-section-of-equity-option-portfolio-returns)

### Inferences
- The lottery-option literature is consistent across 1996-2010 US samples, methods and authors: the further OTM and the more positively skewed the option, the more negative its average return; costs make it worse. Hero-or-zero is the extreme point of that curve (highest moneyness, shortest tenor), so the prior from theory is strongly negative.
- The only retail-level 0DTE P&L study finds net losses concentrated in buying and in transaction costs; there is no published study showing retail 0DTE buying with positive expectancy.
- The volatility-impact papers disagree (Brogaard: volume raises volatility; Dim-Eraker-Vilkov and Adams: gamma lowers intraday volatility; Cboe: hedging negligible), but none of them disputes that 0DTE sellers earn a large volatility risk premium on average.

### Gaps
- Exact per-trade average returns, strike preferences and the buy/sell split in Beckmeyer-Branger-Gayda could not be read (SSRN 403); the $70m vs $125m aggregate figures appear to be different paper vintages but this is not confirmed.
- The size of the 0DTE volatility risk premium in Dim-Eraker-Vilkov and the risk-premium estimates in Bandi-Fusari-Renò are not visible in any accessible summary.
- No study was found covering Indian index-option returns by moneyness (the literature is US single-stock and SPX).

## Key question 3: Expiry-day selling: documented results of 0DTE iron condors, strangles and credit spreads

### Takeaway
Practitioner studies agree that selling 0DTE premium wins most of the time (Option Alpha: 72% for iron butterflies, 63% for iron condors across 25,000+ trades; FlashAlpha: the 10:00 ET SPY straddle contained the close 71% of the time versus 68% fair) and that the premium is modest (median realised/implied move ratio 0.59 versus 0.67 fair, i.e. about 13% overpricing), but results are highly sensitive to entry time and management (morning-entered condors averaged -0.36% versus +37% for afternoon entries), one backtest of a naive 0DTE SPX condor over Jan-Aug 2022 lost at a -60% annualised rate, tail days such as 9 April 2025 (SPY +8.72% against a 3.51% implied move) wipe out weeks of premium, and none of the accessible studies includes commissions and slippage.

### Cited Findings
- Option Alpha (blog first published 18 Aug 2022, updated Feb 2025) [practitioner-firm]: over 25,000 0DTE trades on its automation platform (SPY 88% of volume, 87% opened for a net credit); iron butterflies 72% win rate (4,959 positions), average return +4.62% of initial premium; iron condors 63% (3,170 positions), average +7.94%; condors opened within two hours of the open averaged -0.36%, those opened more than two hours after the open (759 positions) averaged +37% with 67% expiring worthless; 94% of condors held to expiry were full winners; study window, drawdowns and commission treatment not stated. — [Option Alpha](https://optionalpha.com/blog/0dte)
- Option Alpha's live platform statistics across all iron condors (not 0DTE only): 65.06% win rate, average entry 17 DTE. — [Option Alpha stats](https://optionalpha.com/stats)
- Option Alpha video (secondary): a $15,000 P&L difference between 0DTE SPX iron condors opened at 10:30 AM and 2:30 PM. — [Option Alpha video](https://optionalpha.com/videos/morning-vs-afternoon-0dte-backtested-options-strategies)
- Option Omega backtest reproduced by Options Trading IQ [blog/forum]: 0DTE SPX iron condor, January-August 2022, P&L about -$2,684, CAGR -60.30%; author concluded the approach "would need tweaking". — [Options Trading IQ](https://optionstradingiq.com/?p=28544)
- FlashAlpha (Tomasz Dobrowolski, published 21 Jul 2026, updated 13 Sep 2026) [blog/forum, data vendor]: 193 SPY Wednesday sessions July 2022-April 2026; the 10:00 ET ATM straddle's implied 1-sigma band contained the close 71.0% of the time (fair about 68.3%); realised exceeded implied 1-sigma in 25.9% of sessions (fair about 31.7%); median realised/implied ratio 0.589 versus about 0.674 fair ("roughly 13% below fair value"); median implied move 0.72% of spot, median realised 0.37%; hit rate 69-71% in every full year; worst case 9 April 2025, implied 3.51%, SPY closed +8.72% (about 2.5x implied); uses quote mids, excludes fees and slippage, Wednesdays only, FOMC days included; conclusion: a "modest, stable volatility risk premium" and that naked short straddles are "a leverage decision with tail risk". — [FlashAlpha](https://flashalpha.com/articles/are-0dte-straddles-overpriced-193-spy-sessions-data-study)
- tastylive 0DTE studies [practitioner-firm, mostly via third-party digests]: over two years of SPX data, 20-delta short strikes with $10-20 wings were most effective, with the caveat that the uptrend in the test window inflated win rates; strictly selling calls was generally unsuccessful because SPX tends to climb intraday; selling 20-delta put spreads stayed profitable even on down days; a 9:00 AM CT entry (waiting 30 minutes) tended to be best; tastylive itself warns the market "will not always remain favorable for premium selling" and prefers defined risk. — [Recapio digest](https://recapio.com/digest/the-best-time-of-day-to-trade-0dte-options-by-tastylive), [Recapio digest](https://recapio.com/digest/my-trades-were-much-tighter-before-this-study-by-tastylive), [tastylive episode](https://www.tastylive.com/shows/market-measures/episodes/best-times-for-0-dte-scalps-05-08-2025)
- A tastytrade stop-loss study (secondary): about 48% of 0DTE iron condors stopped out at a 0.5x loss would have recovered to breakeven by expiry (benchmark 20-delta condor, $20 wings). — [StockWireX](https://stockwirex.com/?p=47372) [blog/forum]
- A 1DTE SPX short-premium write-up reports distribution and regime effects without a headline return. — [jwplatta](https://jwplatta.github.io/posts/distribution-and-regime-effects-in-a-1dte-options-strategy) [blog/forum]
- Vendor marketing (not evidence): Tachyon advertises a 78.9% win rate and 13.17 profit-to-max-drawdown for an SPX iron condor May 2022-Aug 2026. — [Aeromir](https://futures.aeromir.com/tachyon)
- Cboe: only 4% of SPX 0DTE trading is naked short options; over 95% is limited-risk. — [Cboe Insights](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/0-dt-es-decoded-positioning-trends-and-market-impact)

### Inferences
- The seller's edge on 0DTE is real but thin (about a 3-point hit-rate excess and a 13% median overpricing in the FlashAlpha sample); after a bid-ask spread on four legs plus fees that edge can disappear, which is consistent with Option Alpha's near-zero average for morning condors and the negative Option Omega result.
- Reported win rates of 63-72% come almost entirely from the 2022-2025 US bull market with falling volatility; tastylive itself flags the uptrend bias.
- The main documented risk is not frequency but severity: one 2.5x-implied day in a 193-session sample is enough to erase many weeks of premium for an undefined-risk seller.

### Gaps
- Spintwig's site returned HTTP 403, so none of its SPX/SPY short-dated backtests (which do include commissions) could be read.
- No accessible study reports 0DTE seller P&L net of commissions and slippage; Option Alpha does not state whether fees are included, FlashAlpha explicitly excludes them.
- tastylive's own study pages did not load; the specific win rates and P&L per trade come from third-party digests and should be treated as secondhand.
- No Indian (Nifty/Bank Nifty) expiry-day selling backtest with P&L and costs was found; only index-move statistics.

## Key question 4: Intraday patterns on expiry day: theta decay, pinning / max pain, gamma

### Takeaway
On the last day theta is extreme (tastylive measured a 0DTE SPX ATM straddle losing about 26 cents per $1 of premium between 8:30 and 10:40 CST versus 0.3 cents for a 45-DTE straddle), stock prices measurably cluster at strikes on expiry (Ni-Pearson-Poteshman: optionable-stock returns altered by at least 16.5 bps on average per expiration, attributed to market-maker hedge rebalancing and proprietary manipulation), and the gamma-squeeze / volatility-propagation question is contested in the academic literature, with Cboe reporting negligible net dealer hedging because customer flow is balanced.

### Cited Findings
- tastylive (26 Apr 2023) [practitioner-firm]: measured decay 8:30-10:40 AM CST on the SPX ATM straddle: 0DTE -26 cents per $1 of premium, 45 DTE -0.3 cents; "nearly 100x faster" as a share of premium. — [tastylive](https://www.tastylive.com/news-insights/theta-0dte-options)
- Option Alpha: for a 5-wide SPX put spread 10 points OTM the intraday 0DTE decay curve is not linear across the session. — [Option Alpha](https://optionalpha.com/blog/0dte-options-time-decay) [practitioner-firm]
- FlashAlpha (vendor claim, no methodology shown): 0DTE decay roughly doubles by 2 PM and reaches 4-5x the morning rate by 3:30 PM. — [FlashAlpha](https://flashalpha.com/articles/historical-0dte-data-api-pin-risk-gamma-decay-replay) [blog/forum]
- Ni, Pearson and Poteshman, "Stock price clustering on option expiration dates", Journal of Financial Economics 78(1), 2005, pp. 49-87 [peer-reviewed]: closing prices of optionable stocks cluster at strike prices on expiration dates; returns of optionable stocks are "altered by an average of at least 16.5 basis points" per expiration (about $9 billion of market capitalisation in aggregate); attributed to hedge rebalancing by option market makers and manipulation by proprietary traders; the effect is for single stocks with listed options, not indices. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v78y2005i1p49-87.html), [CXO Advisory summary](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/equity-options/stock-price-pinning-at-options-expiration)
- Ni, Pearson, Poteshman and White, "Does Option Trading Have a Pervasive Impact on Underlying Stock Prices?", Review of Financial Studies 34(4), 2021, extends the hedging-impact result (findings not read). — [EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:oup:rfinst:v:34:y:2021:i:4:p:1952-1986.)
- Gamma and volatility: Dim-Eraker-Vilkov find higher 0DTE gamma associated with lower intraday realised volatility; Adams et al. find volatility about 60 annualised bps lower on 0DTE days with market-maker net gamma staying positive through the day; Brogaard-Han-Won find higher 0DTE volume associated with about 14% higher daily volatility; Cboe (May 2025) finds net dealer gamma hedging at most 0.2% of SPX daily liquidity because customer flow is balanced. — [Harbourfront](https://harbourfrontquant.substack.com/p/the-rise-of-0dte-options-cause-for), [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/do-sp500-0dtes-options-increase-market-volatility/), [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/zero-day-options-are-reordering-the-way-the-stock-market-behaves-1.1921930), [Cboe Insights](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/0-dt-es-decoded-positioning-trends-and-market-impact)
- SpotGamma (vendor) claims 0DTE realised volatility runs 3-5x the underlying's normal daily volatility because of gamma concentration; no published methodology. — [SpotGamma](https://spotgamma.com/?p=19793) [blog/forum]
- Crypto pinning: a crypto news site cites an unnamed study that spot moved toward max pain in about 60% of cases in the 24 hours before settlement; another notes the June 2025 $15bn Deribit quarterly expiry (max pain $102,000, put-call volume ratio 0.75, IV lowest since October 2023) saw bitcoin "barely budge". These are [blog/forum] claims. — [Cryptometer](https://cryptometer.io/news/massive-options-expiry-set-to-stir-bitcoin-and-ether-markets), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/360072/deribit-tops-40b-in-bitcoin-options-open-interest-amid-15b-quarterly-expiry)

### Inferences
- The hero-or-zero buyer is fighting a decay rate on the order of a quarter of the premium per two morning hours (US data) and a documented tendency of underlyings to settle near strikes; both work against far-OTM longs, which need the price to leave the pinned zone.
- The academic disagreement on gamma propagation means a "gamma squeeze" cannot be relied on as a systematic source of hero payoffs; the one paper that finds higher volatility (Brogaard) links it to speculative volume, not to a mechanical dealer-hedging loop.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study of pinning or max pain on index options (Nifty/Bank Nifty/SPX) was found; Ni-Pearson-Poteshman is single-stock.
- No rigorous intraday realised-versus-implied study of SPX 0DTE beyond vendor work (FlashAlpha) was found.
- The crypto max-pain "60%" statistic has no identifiable primary source.

## Key question 5: Crypto daily options: what the Deribit / Delta Exchange India evidence says

### Takeaway
Daily BTC/ETH options have existed on Deribit since February 2020 (08:00 UTC settlement on a 07:30-08:00 UTC index TWAP) and on Delta Exchange India (17:30 IST settlement on a 30-minute index TWAP, D1/D2 chains relaunched daily at 17:30 IST), and academic work on Deribit data finds a bitcoin variance risk premium far larger than equities (0.14 in annualised-variance units versus about 0.02 for the S&P 500 in Almeida et al., 2017-2022; Bybit/Block Scholes report implied volatility at about 1.5x realised in 2025), but no study or backtest of daily (0DTE/1DTE) BTC option returns, buyer or seller, was found anywhere.

### Cited Findings

**Contract facts [practitioner-firm, exchange documentation]**
- Deribit began daily BTC index options on 3 February 2020, with daily options tradable from 08:00 UTC the day before expiry (initial lifetime two trading days) and "similar contract specifications to the other options"; daily ETH options followed. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/news/deribit-to-offer-daily-bitcoin-options/), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/deribit-to-launch-daily-btc-options-as-regulated-competition-heats-up), [Finance Magnates (ETH)](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/exchange/deribit-adds-ethereum-contracts-to-daily-options-offering/)

- Deribit launched USDC-settled linear BTC/ETH options on 19 August 2025 alongside inverse options; a Deribit exchange update titled "0 bps Maker Fees on Linear Daily Options" is dated 2 November 2026 in the Insights index (title only seen). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/367106/deribit-linear-bitcoin-ether-options), [Deribit Insights index](https://insights.deribit.com/?p=2318)

- CME has announced daily expirations on bitcoin and ether futures options (details not read). — [Yahoo Finance](https://it.finance.yahoo.com/news/cme-add-daily-expirations-bitcoin-140946855.html)

**Volatility risk premium in bitcoin options [peer-reviewed]**

- Market structure: Deribit held nearly 90% of BTC options open interest in June 2025 ($40bn+, $15bn quarterly expiry); by early 2026 one source puts Deribit's share below 39% as IBIT options grew; Deribit BTC options OI $31.3bn in May 2026. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/360072/deribit-tops-40b-in-bitcoin-options-open-interest-amid-15b-quarterly-expiry), [CoinPerps](https://www.coinperps.com/learn/crypto-options-statistics) [blog/forum]

### Inferences
- The structural condition that makes expiry-day selling profitable on average in equities (implied above realised) is present and larger in bitcoin options according to both academic (VRP 0.14 vs 0.02) and practitioner (IV about 1.5x RV) sources, but every measurement is at 7-90-day tenors; nothing confirms it at 0-1 day.
- Almeida et al.'s finding that OTM bitcoin calls earned a positive premium on average (2017-2022, a period that included two major bull runs) is the one piece of evidence that partly cuts against the equity lottery-option result for calls; it is a risk-premium interpretation on monthly options, not a strategy return, and the same paper finds OTM puts expensive.
- Delta India's D1 ladder (200-point steps for BTC, strikes added every five minutes to keep the 0.2-0.8 delta band) means truly far-OTM "hero" strikes are listed only while the chain is young or after a large move; the listing rule itself limits the lottery-ticket supply relative to Nifty's fixed ladder.
- The Delta settlement-fee cap at 5-10% of premium and the exemption for options expiring worthless mean the cost burden falls on winners and on the seller side, which is the opposite of NSE where STT falls on the exercised buyer.

### Gaps
- No backtest, academic paper, exchange report or vendor note on returns to buying or selling daily BTC/ETH options (Deribit or Delta) was found; this is the central missing evidence.
- No public figure for daily expiries' share of Deribit or Delta volume, or for retail participation in crypto daily options, was found.
- Deribit's current daily-option lifetime (two days per the 2020 BTC launch versus 24 hours per the 2020 ETH report) and the content of the November 2026 "linear daily options" fee update were not verified.
- Delta India's exact current option fee and premium cap conflict across official pages (0.015% vs 0.03%; 10% vs 12.5% cap).

## Key question 6: Does the 24/7 market and the 12:00 UTC (17:30 IST) settlement on Delta India change the picture?

### Takeaway
Bitcoin volatility and liquidity are documented to be lowest in the mid-Asian session and highest during the European/US overlap (roughly 13:00-18:00 UTC, liquidity depth about 40% higher than Asian hours per Kaiko Q4 2023), so Deribit's 08:00 UTC settlement falls in the European morning after the quiet Asian session while Delta India's 12:00 UTC settlement sits in the European afternoon just before the most volatile US window; both venues settle on a 30-minute TWAP with no overnight gap, which removes the gap-risk argument against selling but also removes the overnight catalyst that often drives Indian hero-or-zero payoffs.

### Cited Findings
- Wang et al. (2020, Finance Research Letters, Bitstamp data) [peer-reviewed]: the bitcoin market shows "clearly higher trading volume and volatility during hours overlapping European and US stock exchange daytime sessions", while the Asian stock-market open "only marginally affects volatility". — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v34y2020ics1544612319301904.html)
- An arXiv study of Bitstamp/Kraken high-frequency data finds a rise in activity at 00:00 UTC (Asian start), a decline until the European open at 06:00 UTC, and markedly calmer weekends. — [arXiv 2306.17095](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.17095) [peer-reviewed working paper]
- Catania and Sandholdt (2019): intraday volatility and volume patterns differ between a Europe-based exchange (Bitstamp) and a US-based one (Coinbase), tracking local time of day. — [arXiv 2109.12142](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.12142v2.pdf)
- A TRP study reported by Blockworks: UTC hours 2 and 15-18 were the most volatile; Asian volatility lasts five to six hours before fading. — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/bitcoins-price-predictable-study) [blog/forum]
- Arcane Research (2022): returns weakest during Asian hours, most erratic price movements during US market hours (one year of data). — [CoinMarketCap Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/bitcoin-s-performance-varies-hugely-depending-on-time-of-day-research-suggests) [blog/forum]
- Kaiko Research (Q4 2023, via AltIndex): bitcoin liquidity depth on leading platforms is about 40% higher during the 13:00-17:00 UTC US-Europe overlap than during Asian hours; mid-Asian session ranked lowest volatility, New York open / London close overlap highest. — [AltIndex](https://altindex.com/news/intraday-trading-anomolies-bitcoin-volatility) [blog/forum]
- Settlement mechanics: Deribit 07:30-08:00 UTC TWAP; Delta India 30-minute TWAP to 17:30 IST (12:00 UTC). — [Deribit Settlement](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/29734325712413-Settlement), [Delta Exchange India options guide](https://guides.delta.exchange/delta-exchange-india-user-guide/derivatives-guide/options-guide.md)
- For comparison, SEBI's measures target the 15:30 IST NSE close, and SEBI's Jane Street order describes index moves engineered within the 09:15-15:30 IST cash session. — [ECGI blog](https://www.ecgi.global/publications/blog/expiry-day-and-the-governance-of-algorithmic-trading-the-jane-street-episode)

### Inferences
- A Delta D1 option's final hours (roughly 08:00-12:00 UTC) span the European session, with the US cash open (13:30 UTC) after settlement; a Deribit daily's final hours (04:00-08:00 UTC) span the quietest part of the day. On the session-volatility evidence, Delta's window should carry more realised movement than Deribit's but less than a contract settling in US hours; this is an inference from session studies, not a measured property of either contract.
- Because crypto trades 24/7 and settles on a 30-minute TWAP, the Indian expiry-day dynamics that depend on a cash-market open/close (overnight gap at 09:15, "soft close" at 15:30, constituent-stock buying to move the index) have no direct analogue; the TWAP also dampens last-minute spikes that hero-or-zero buyers rely on in the final minutes before 15:30 IST.
- The absence of a trading halt also removes the overnight-gap argument that makes 1DTE equity selling riskier than 0DTE; the new D1 chain opens at 17:30 IST immediately after the prior one settles, so a Delta seller is continuously exposed with no gap but also no forced pause.
- Whether the much larger bitcoin VRP survives at the daily tenor, and whether the Delta D1 ladder's 0.2-0.8 delta range even lists the far-OTM strikes a hero-or-zero buyer wants, are both unverified; the honest conclusion is that the equity evidence (strong negative expectancy for far-OTM buyers, thin and tail-exposed positive expectancy for defined-risk sellers after costs) is the best available prior for crypto dailies, with no crypto-specific confirmation or contradiction found.

### Gaps
- No study measures implied versus realised volatility or option returns specifically over the Delta 08:00-12:00 UTC or Deribit 04:00-08:00 UTC final windows.
- No evidence on whether the 12:00 UTC TWAP window itself attracts manipulation or pinning behaviour on Delta India.
- Session-volatility studies are 2019-2023 vintage; post-ETF (2024-2026) intraday patterns may differ and were not found in peer-reviewed form.
